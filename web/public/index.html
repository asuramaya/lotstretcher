How it works

Three steps, and nothing in between them touches a server.

1

Add photos

Pick a folder, take a photo, or paste image URLs from a listing you already have open.

2

It sorts and cuts

Exterior shots are separated from interiors, the background is removed, and each angle is identified — front, side, rear.

3

Take the bundle

Composed images plus post copy for Facebook, Instagram and Threads, as a single download or straight to your share sheet.

The backdrop comes from the car

There is no stock background library, and that is deliberate. The backdrop behind each vehicle is generated from that vehicle's own paint and interior colors, so no two listings share one. A single repeated backdrop is what gets a feed flagged as spam.

It needs no API key, no downloaded assets and no network call. It is the default because it is the best available answer, not because it is the free tier of something.

What actually leaves your device

Your photos: nothing. Not "encrypted in transit", not "deleted after 30 days" — there is no server to send them to. lotstretcher.org is static files. The only requests this page makes are for its own code, a one-time model download, and any image URLs you paste yourself.
