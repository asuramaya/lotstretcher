No stock backgrounds

The backdrop comes from the car.

Each backdrop is generated from that vehicle's own paint and interior colours: a gradient, a sweep, a halo or two tones of it. No two listings share one, so a feed never looks stamped out.

No API key. No network call.

The studio

Then make it yours.

Five tools on one stage, every lever redrawing a live preview as you move it. The stage is the still you will post, in the shape you have picked.

  • Backdrop. The car's own gradient, a stock picture like this flag, or your own lot.
  • Frame. A line the core draws, a stock frame, or your dealer's, fitted to every shape.
  • Text. Title, subtitle and price, each with its own font, corner and colour.
  • Light. A spotlight with strength and spread, a glow, a ground shadow, a floor reflection.
  • Video. Length, pace and the shapes of the clip.

Still on your device. The backdrops, frames and fonts are a few hundred KB of files shipped with the site.

Text

Three pieces, each its own.

The title, a subtitle and the price badge. Set them together or one at a time: font, corner, colour, case, on a pill or bare. The badge can take the vehicle's paint. Drag any piece on the stage and it snaps to a corner.

  • Six fonts, all open licensed, drawn by the same core on the still and the clip
  • Pieces sharing a corner stack; pieces in different corners keep clear of each other
  • A title too wide for the shape shrinks to one line before it wraps
Video

The clip, from the same choices.

Three or more exteriors become a conveyor: each shot rolls into the hero slot and out again, on the same backdrop, in the same frame, with the same text and light as the stills. The arrangement follows the shape: a lineup across a horizontal clip, a stack down a portrait one.

Window sticker

Specs, without typing them.

Point it at a window sticker PDF and it reads the trim, engine, colours and options straight off the page.

  • VIN, trim and drivetrain
  • Factory colour names, which set the backdrop
  • Optional equipment, into the post copy
Library

Everything it made, in one place.

The pipeline writes one folder per vehicle. The Library pane reads that folder and shows every still, framed set, clip and post, searchable by title, VIN or stock number.

  • Open a listings folder from disk, on any browser
  • Load a vehicle's originals back in and rerun it with today's choices
  • On your server: rebuild in place, re-scrape from the listing, sync the lot, delist

Nothing leaves the device.

Not encrypted in transit. Not deleted after 30 days. There is no server to send photos to. This site is static files, and the work happens in your tab.
