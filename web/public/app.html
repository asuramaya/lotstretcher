Paste image URLs

One per line. The photos are fetched straight from the dealer's CDN to this device. Nothing passes through lotstretcher.org. A CDN that refuses cross-origin reads will fail, and the app says which.

Window sticker URL

A direct link to the sticker PDF. It is fetched straight to this device and parsed here. Ford's sticker host allows this; some others refuse cross-origin reads, and the app will say so.

About

lotstretcher turns vehicle photos into post-ready images and copy. Background removal, classification and compositing all run in this browser tab, on this device.

What leaves this device

Your photos: nothing. There is no account, no upload, and no server-side storage, because the site is static files. The only network requests are the one-time model download and any image URLs you paste.
