About

lotstretcher turns vehicle photos into post-ready images and copy. Everything — background removal, classification, compositing — runs in this browser tab on this device.

What leaves this device

Your photos: nothing. There is no account, no upload, and no server-side storage — the site is static files. The only network requests are the one-time model download, and fetching any image URLs you paste.
